How it works

A simple model that is easy for guests and hosts.

This concept is designed to be easy to demo, easy to explain, and easy to launch: a guest sees a flyer, scans a code, selects a package, and the experience is delivered directly to the rental.

1

Host displays flyer or QR card

The host only needs a clean in-unit flyer or countertop card with package options and a scan-to-order CTA.

2

Guest chooses a themed upgrade

Beach, fishing, romance, spa, breakfast, or recovery—simple products with clear value and emotional appeal.

3

Delivery completes the experience

Packages can be delivered before arrival, staged for special occasions, or handled same-day for urgent add-ons like recovery kits.

For Airbnb and vacation rental hosts

A guest amenity that can elevate reviews and add revenue.

This service is designed to be a low-friction host partnership. Hosts get an easy amenity story, guests get memorable add-ons, and the ordering flow is handled externally.
